# Verification — thyroid function and cholesterol

Checked 2026-11-21. 34 claims: 21 confirmed, 13 partly, 0 not supported, 0 not accessible. Of these, 30 are the threads' load-bearing claims; C04.7 and C05.7 were added when the report was written, and C03.7 and C05.8 when it was rewritten to include thread 03; those four were checked by the report writers, not by a separate checker.

Each claim was checked by an agent that did not write it, one checker per thread. A checker opened the cited source, or one other route to it, and reported what it says. "Partly" means the figures matched and the wording did not; every such claim was corrected in its thread file, and the Statement column shows the corrected wording. No figure in any claim was found to be wrong.

Thread 03 (direct effects of TSH) was written later the same day, after its first research agent was stopped by a model's safety filter. Its six claims were checked by a separate agent and are in the table.
