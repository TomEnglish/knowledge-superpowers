Each claim was checked by an agent that did not write it, one checker per thread. A checker opened the cited source, or one other route to it, and reported what it says. "Partly" means the figures matched and something in the wording did not; every such claim was corrected in its thread file, and the Statement column shows the corrected wording. No figure in any claim was found to be wrong.

## Corrections made
- C01.3: the thread said ALLHAT-LLT was "funded under a US government contract"; its funding note also lists financial support from Pfizer and pravastatin supplied by Bristol-Myers Squibb. Corrected in `01-statins-all-cause-mortality.md` in three places.
- C02.1: the thread called the VESALIUS-CV mortality result "clearly fewer deaths", and said that it was formally exploratory rested on a search snippet. The checker read the analysis plan on the registry record and the US label: death from any cause came ninth in a fixed testing order after a non-significant coronary-death result, so the result is nominal. Corrected in `02-other-lipid-drugs-and-diet-trials.md`, and the label added as source [02.86].
- C03.1: the reference category (3.4 to 3.9 mmol/L) for the hazard ratio was added. Corrected in `03-cohorts-and-genetics.md`.
- C03.5: the PCSK9 estimates were attributed to Copenhagen; they are for Copenhagen and UK Biobank combined. Both are now given, with the paper's two UK Biobank counts. Corrected in `03-cohorts-and-genetics.md`.
- C04.1: "must go to" changed to "should be made directly to", the source's wording. Corrected in `04-disputes-and-conflicts.md`.
- C04.3: the thread described the 2022 meta-analysis as "co-authored by statin critics", which the paper does not say. It now states that one co-author published a 2018 reappraisal of the lipid hypothesis, citing that paper, and that mortality was pooled in 19 of the 21 trials. Corrected in `04-disputes-and-conflicts.md` in three places.
- Not a checked claim: the disputes thread carried an unconfirmed statement, from a search result, about a named author's outside work. It was removed before verification because it could not be confirmed at source.
- C02.8 (report writer's check): the thread gave the VESALIUS-CV entry criterion as LDL of at least 90 mg/dL; the registry also allows entry on non-HDL cholesterol or apoB. Corrected in `02-other-lipid-drugs-and-diet-trials.md` in three places.
- C04.7 (report writer's check): the thread listed "that the two critics sued were dishonest" among claims refuted. The court did not decide that; truth was not tried and the claim settled. Corrected in `04-disputes-and-conflicts.md`.

## Absence claims
A source cannot confirm that something does not exist. These rest on the searches listed in each thread's Gaps section, as of 2026-11-21.
- No non-statin cholesterol-lowering method has shown fewer deaths from any cause as a trial's planned main result (thread 02).
- No outcome-trial result for inclisiran or obicetrapib had been published (thread 02; ClinicalTrials.gov records checked).
- PREVENTABLE had posted no results (thread 01; registry record checked).
- Arm-level death counts could not be found for several older trials (thread 02, Gaps).

## Access limits
NEJM, JAMA, the Lancet, the AHA journals, OUP, Wiley and NCBI Bookshelf returned bot checks, which were not worked around. Those sources were read through PubMed, Europe PMC, PubMed Central, ClinicalTrials.gov results records, US drug labels and Internet Archive copies. Where that gave only an abstract, the Checked column says so. The full VESALIUS-CV papers were not read by anyone in this run.
